# Qualitative Analysis

Methods for collecting and processing non-numerical data including interviews, observations,
and open-ended text - to understand meaning, experience, and context behind agent behavior.

---

## affinity mapping

**Definition**: qualitative research method for organizing and grouping related ideas or
observations; participants sort data points - notes, quotes, themes - into clusters based on
natural relationships

**Purpose**: commonly used in UX research and design thinking to synthesize findings into
identifiable patterns

**Related Terms**: [codebook]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#codebook), [qualitative research]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#qualitative-research), [synthesis]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#synthesis)

---

## codebook

**Definition**: structured guide used in qualitative research to categorize and tag data
consistently; defines categories, codes, and rules for applying them to text or observations

**Purpose**: ensures systematic analysis across multiple researchers or datasets

**Related Terms**: [Cohen's kappa coefficient]({{< relref "/research/quantitative" >}}#cohens-kappa-coefficient), [qualitative research]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#qualitative-research), [synthesis]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#synthesis)

---

## dissemination

**Definition**: systematic sharing of research findings with target audiences beyond the research
team; requires planning for audience, timing, and appropriate communication channels

**Purpose**: ensures knowledge can advance the field, change practice and policy, or inform future
research

**Example**: journal publications, conference presentations, social media, press releases, websites

**Related Terms**: [synthesis]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#synthesis)

---

## qualitative research

**Definition**: method focused on understanding meaning, experiences, and context through
non-numerical data; produces insights about _"why"_ and _"how"_ rather than _"how many"_

**Purpose**: collects data through interviews, observations, open-ended surveys, and document
analysis to surface context that numerical data alone can't capture

**Related Terms**: [affinity mapping]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#affinity-mapping), [codebook]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#codebook), [Cohen's kappa coefficient]({{< relref "/research/quantitative" >}}#cohens-kappa-coefficient), [synthesis]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#synthesis)

---

## synthesis

**Definition**: process of combining multiple research findings or data points into coherent
insights; transforms raw observations into patterns, themes, and actionable conclusions

**Purpose**: critical step between data collection and decision-making

**Related Terms**: [affinity mapping]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#affinity-mapping), [dissemination]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#dissemination), [qualitative research]({{< relref "/research/qualitative" >}}#qualitative-research)
